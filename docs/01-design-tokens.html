Typography

Three roles, three faces. Fraunces carries personality in headlines only; Plex Sans is the workhorse body face; Plex Mono renders every macro/micro number in tabular figures — a deliberate nod to the nutrition-label format this app is built around.

DISPLAYfont-family: 'Fraunces', Georgia, serif — weight 600–700

Protein target reached.

BODYfont-family: 'IBM Plex Sans', sans-serif — weight 400–600

Your dinner has been adjusted based on today's workout — 180 more kcal, 12g more protein.

DATAfont-family: 'IBM Plex Mono', monospace — tabular-nums, weight 500–600

24g / 90g protein
